# PlugV brand assets

- `logo-horizontal.svg`: primary wordmark for dark surfaces.
- `logo-horizontal-light.svg`: primary wordmark for light surfaces.
- `logo-icon.svg`: scalable standalone mark.
- `app-icon-1024.png`: iOS and app-store master icon.
- `android-icon-512.png` and `android-icon-192.png`: Android/PWA icon sizes.
- `apple-touch-icon.png`: Apple home-screen icon.
- `favicon.svg` and `favicon-32.png`: browser icons.
- `plugv-social-card.png`: social and Open Graph sharing image.

Keep clear space around the mark equal to at least one quarter of the icon width. Do not recolour, stretch, rotate, add effects, or place the wordmark on a low-contrast background. Use the icon alone only where the full PlugV name cannot fit.
