# PlugV Knowledge Hub — Instagram Launch Series

Account: @plugvplatform  
Website destination: https://plugv.in/knowledge

## Publishing principles

- One useful claim per slide; avoid dense paragraphs.
- Use 1080 × 1350 for carousels and keep critical text inside a 120 px safe margin.
- Cite the PlugV guide in the caption and place “Source checked” on policy content.
- Never publish a subsidy amount without rechecking the governing notification on the publication date.
- End every post with one relevant action, not multiple competing calls to action.

## Seven-post launch sequence

### 1. Reel — EV vs petrol: what actually costs less?

Hook: “The cheaper car may cost more after 5 years.”  
Frames: Purchase price → annual kilometres → energy → maintenance → resale → result.  
CTA: “Use your own numbers in the free PlugV calculator. Link in bio.”

Caption: A price tag is only the beginning. PlugV’s calculator compares EV and petrol ownership using your annual driving, charging rate, fuel price, maintenance, insurance and resale assumptions. The result is an estimate—not a quotation. Try it at plugv.in/knowledge.

### 2. Carousel — EV subsidies: verify before you buy

Slides: Cover → vehicle category → policy validity → road tax → registration → dealer quotation → official portal checklist.  
CTA: “Save this before requesting an EV quotation.”

Caption: EV incentives can depend on category, registration date, policy caps and remaining allocation. Do not rely on an old post or an unqualified discount figure. Use PlugV’s verification guide and confirm the current government notification.

### 3. Carousel — Your first EV in five decisions

Slides: Weekly travel → home charging → range buffer → exact variant → test the ownership journey.  
CTA: “Send this to someone choosing their first EV.”

### 4. Reel — Home charging vs public charging

Hook: “Which charger should an EV owner actually use?”  
Resolution: Home for routine energy; public fast charging for travel and backup; the right mix depends on access and tariff.  
CTA: “Read the complete charging guide on PlugV.”

### 5. Carousel — EV TCO in 30 seconds

Slides: Price + energy + maintenance + insurance − resale = TCO.  
CTA: “Calculate, don’t guess.”

### 6. Carousel — Five EV myths that need context

Slides: Claimed range → rain → fast charging → maintenance → highway travel.  
CTA: “Comment with the EV claim you want PlugV to verify next.”

### 7. Carousel — Top EVs by category

Slides: SUVs → hatchbacks → sedans → MPVs → crossovers → how PlugV orders the list.  
CTA: “Open the live category list—vehicle data can change.”
